## Task 7: Revenue Seasonality and Monthly Trends

## Task 8: Gross Margin Compression Diagnostic
The decline in overall company gross margin from 34.7% to 33.0% was not caused by falling product profitability. Gross margins within individual product lines remained stable (House Blend at ~38.3%, Single Origin at ~44.3%, and Bulk Commercial at ~19.9%). Instead, the compression resulted from a product mix shift: Bulk Commercial, the lowest-margin product line at 19.91%,grew from 25.02% of total revenue in FY2024 (R110.34 million) to 33.65% in FY2025 (R161.50 million), reducing the overall weighted margin.
